SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIAL
======================

This package accompanies the anonymous submission "What Form Cannot Tell:
Resemblance, the Intermediate Stage, and Wiktionary's Inherited and Learned
Labels in 17 Languages". It contains no author names, affiliations or links.

Contents
--------
index.html
    One standalone web page. Open it in any browser. It needs no network
    connection, no server and no other file. It has no tracking or analytics.

supplementary_material.txt
    This file.

What the page shows
-------------------
Overview          The question, four headline figures and the pipeline diagram.
Question          What a classifier sees (the modern word and the ancestor
                  form) and what was hoped for against what was found.
Data and setup    The 17 language pairs, the sample of 13,128 pages, the
                  12,778 items, the three baseline models and the definition
                  of congruent and dissociated (hard) items.
Results           Macro-F1 per language for the distance-only model and the
                  full form-based model. Accuracy on congruent and hard items
                  with the majority rate. The recount for 15 threshold pairs.
Hard items        Three inputs for three models (logistic regression, a
                  character Transformer, a pretrained character model) with
                  balanced accuracy. What raises accuracy on the hard items
                  (training weights, word meaning, a dictionary record) and
                  how far. The outside checks.
Label history     How many labels named a different route two years earlier,
                  and when and by how many accounts the changed labels were set.
Code              Eight excerpts from the scripts that produced the results,
                  unchanged, each with a copy button.
Reproducibility   Environment, seeds and what can and cannot be rerun.
Limits            The main limits of the study.

Relation to the paper
---------------------
The page expands the paper and makes no claim that the paper does not make.
Every number on it is read by a script from the result files of the corrected
run, or, for the pretrained model, from the printed summary of that run. The
tables give per-language and per-condition figures for which the paper has
only ranges or pooled values.

Code excerpts
-------------
normalised edit distance; normalisation of the scripts to Devanagari; the
features of the three baseline models; the congruent and dissociated split;
the reading of one etymology section per sense; training weights by route and
distance band; meaning features from definition lines; the threshold grid.
They are excerpts, not a runnable package.

Environment
-----------
Python with numpy 2.5.4, scipy 1.18.1 and scikit-learn 1.9.1 for everything
except the two neural models. Seed 20261011 for folds and models. The
character Transformer was trained with 5 seeds and the pretrained model with
3. Hardware for the pretrained model: one GPU. Other details of the neural
runs are in the paper's appendix.

Reproducibility notes and known limits
--------------------------------------
- The experiments cannot be reproduced end to end from this package. It holds
  excerpts and results, not the page texts or the full scripts.
- The analyses that read the comparative dictionary used a machine-readable
  copy that carries no licence. It was used for analysis only, and nothing
  from it is included or released.
- Per-item predictions of the pretrained model are not available. Its figures
  come from the printed summary of the run.
- The labels studied are not validated. Scores measure agreement with the
  labels, not historical accuracy.
- The split into congruent and dissociated items is defined by a feature of
  the model and is a diagnostic, not an independent test set.
