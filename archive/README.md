# Archive

Nothing here is deleted. These directories hold the project's earlier states.

## `sycophancy_study_2026-10-10/`

The multilingual sycophancy study (RQ1/RQ2 on cross-lingual false corrections). It is retained as historical work and is **not** the active research direction.

- **Status at archiving:** pilot on hold; no experiments run; Results empty.
- **Contents:** the full `research.md` (v2 with edits), literature files, `configs/languages.yaml` (Glottolog-verified panel), all study docs (decision memo, preregistration draft, translation-validation protocol, cross-family extension, earlier `research.md` versions), and the `sycoling/` code with its tests.
- **Running the archived tests:** `cd archive/sycophancy_study_2026-10-10 && python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -t .`
- `docs/decision_log.md` and `docs/feasibility.md` in this directory are copies taken before the active versions were rewritten. The active decision log continues in `docs/decision_log.md`, with entries 1–30 unchanged and new entries appended.

## `superseded_2026-10-10/`

`lost_linguistic_links_proposal.md` was the first draft of the new direction (withheld-stage recovery). It was superseded after the full-text audit: recovering intermediate or ancestral stages is substantially addressed by Bouchard-Côté et al. 2013 and Cathcart & Rama 2020 (decision log 31).

It also contains one citation error, corrected in the active files: the borrowing paper is **List & Forkel 2021** (Open Research Europe 1:79), not "Forkel & List 2022".
